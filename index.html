Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I add too much pigment to epoxy resin?
Adding more than 5% pigment by weight disrupts the precise stoichiometric ratio of epoxy resin to polyamine hardener. The excess unbound pigment molecules inhibit cross-linking, resulting in a soft, flexible, sticky, or permanently tacky surface.
Can I use food coloring or water-based acrylic paint?
No. Water is the mortal enemy of epoxy. Introducing water-based food coloring or wet acrylic paints introduces moisture that causes microscopic foaming, bubbles, cloudiness, and compromised tensile strength.
How do I calculate mica powder for river tables?
A loading ratio of 1.5% to 2.0% of total resin weight delivers a shimmering, opaque river effect that prevents seeing the underlying plywood base. For a 2-gallon pour (~17 lbs of resin), you will need roughly 110 to 150 grams of mica powder.
